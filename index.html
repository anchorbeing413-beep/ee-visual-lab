The EE Visual Lab Methodology

01. Physical Intuition PHYSICS

Understand what electrons, displacement currents, and electric flux lines are actually doing inside the component before touching mathematical formulas.

02. Exact Derivation MATHEMATICS

Derive governing equations from first principles—Kirchhoff’s laws, constitutive relations, differential equations, and boundary conditions.

03. Interactive Simulation LABORATORY

Dynamically manipulate parameters ($R, C, V$) on high-precision dual-trace oscilloscopes to observe settling times, time constants ($\tau$), and energy dissipation in real time.

04. Reverse Engineering ANALYSIS

Given an observed transient response or rise-time metric, work backwards to diagnose and calculate underlying physical circuit parameters.

Physical Verification Notes

At t = 0^+, voltage continuity requires v_C(0^+) = v_C(0^-). All source voltage momentarily drops across R, delivering peak current I_{max} = (V_s - V_0)/R.

Settling Criterion: An RC circuit is considered practically settled at t \ge 5\tau (99.33% of step value).
