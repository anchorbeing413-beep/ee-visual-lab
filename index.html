The EE Visual Lab Educational Methodology

01. Physical Intuition PHYSICS

Understand what displacement currents, charge accumulation, and dielectric polarization are actually doing inside the component before touching formulas.

02. Exact Derivation MATHEMATICS

Derive governing equations from first principles—Kirchhoff’s laws, constitutive relations, differential equations, and continuous boundary conditions.

03. Interactive Simulation LABORATORY

Dynamically manipulate parameters ($R, C, V_s$) on calibrated dual-trace oscilloscopes to observe time constants ($\tau$), rise times, and energy conservation.

04. Reverse Engineering ANALYSIS

Given an observed transient response or rise-time metric, work backwards to diagnose and calculate underlying physical circuit parameters.

Physical Verification Notes

At t = 0^+, voltage continuity requires v_C(0^+) = v_C(0^-). All source voltage momentarily drops across R, delivering peak inrush current I_{\text{max}} = (V_s - V_0)/R.

Settling Criterion: An RC circuit is considered practically settled at t \ge 5\tau (99.33% of step value).
